# Connect your assistant

The directory is used through a **skill**: a package of instructions that tells your assistant where the index lives, how to pick stores and how to query each one with its own tools. You install no service of ours and share no data with us.

Each assistant has its own way of installing skills and its own browsing capabilities. We label honestly what we have tested and what is pending.

:::clients
:::

## Before you start

- Your assistant must be able to **read web pages**. Without browsing it cannot query the stores.
- Results depend on each store: some load products with JavaScript or block automated access. The assistant will tell you when it could not check a store.
- The directory starts with stores in Uruguay. If your country is not there yet, you can [propose stores](../merchants/).

## Problems?

Open a report in the [plugin repository](https://github.com/ignaciolarranaga/open-commerce-plugin/issues/new/choose) with your assistant, what you asked and what it answered.
