# Terms of use

Last updated: 31 October 2026.

## What this is

OpenCommerceIndex is an open, volunteer-maintained directory that describes online stores and how an assistant can query them. It is information, not a shopping service. Purchases, payments, delivery and warranties happen exclusively between you and the store.

## No warranties or recommendations

The directory and the plugin are provided "as is", with no warranty of accuracy, availability or fitness for a purpose. A profile describes how to find information on a store; it is not a recommendation, a verification of the seller's commercial reliability or a statement about prices. Prices and stock shown by your assistant are retrieved live from the store's site and can change or contain errors. Always confirm on the store before buying.

## Relationship with stores

We have no commercial relationship with listed stores, charge nothing for listing or ranking and use no affiliate links. Store names and content belong to their owners. Any store can request a correction or removal.
